LatentFill settings

Everything is stored only in this browser. Your resume and form questions go straight from your browser to the AI provider you choose.

1. AI provider

Leave blank for the default shown. A small, fast model works well and costs fractions of a cent per application.

2. Resume

No file saved.

Optional if you uploaded a PDF. Parsing fills this in for you.

3. Standard answers

Questions your resume can't answer. Leave anything blank to have it skipped and flagged for you.

Voluntary self-identification (EEO)

Blank = "Decline to self-identify" where that option exists.

Cover letter

When an application asks for a cover letter, autofill writes one in your format, tailored to that job, and attaches it as a PDF. It's saved here so you can review or edit it. You can also open a job in another tab and generate one manually.

4. Profile (advanced)

The structured data the AI fills forms from. Created by "Parse resume", and you can edit it directly.

5. Memory

LatentFill keeps learning. Facts you teach it and answers you correct or submit are saved here and used on every future application, and they take priority over the resume.
